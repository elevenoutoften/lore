Reading long-form knowledge in the same dark, dense chrome used for browsing made pages feel like log output. The reader now gets a warm, paper-like surface that signals “this is a document.”

## Why

Curators spend minutes per page, not seconds. A calmer reading surface measurably improved review focus in testing, while [[services/lore|Lore]]’s browse and graph stay on the dark system.
