Lanes keep retrieval scoped. A companion-lane question should not pull ops runbooks, and a draft should not leak into project answers.

## The lanes

- **project** — canonical project knowledge.
- **procedural** — how-to and runbooks.
- **ops** — operational state.
- **companion** — conversational memory.
- **draft** — unreviewed.
